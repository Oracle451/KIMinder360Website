KIMinder360 Mobile App

Demo

Below are some of the key prototyped UI features of the KIMinder360 app.

Home Screen

The app home screen provides quick access to active hours and category settings. A list of recently completed directives will be added below these settings to easily view your training progress.

Category Selection

Users can choose directive categories to tailor training to their goals. The automated directives will then be randomly chosen from those active categories.

Active Hours

Users can set action windows for each day to control when directives are delivered, reducing interruptions outside preferred times.
