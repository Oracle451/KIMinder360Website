KIMinder360 Mobile App

Feature Demonstration

Below are the key features of the KIMinder360 app, each component is designed to give users full control over their situational awareness training experience. Next to the descriptions of each feature you will find a screenshot or video demonstrating what the implementation looks like in the app.

Home Screen

Your central hub for everything KIMinder360.

The Home Screen is the central hub for all user activity. From here, users can quickly jump into any part of the app by adjusting their active hours, browsing and toggling directive categories, updating personal settings, and reviewing the directives that have recently been sent to them.

The recent directives list at the bottom of the screen lets users quickly respond to the directives that have most recently been sent to them. From this list they can also view their current streak (if any exists) as well as access the complete directive history screen.

  • Quick access to Active Hours and Category settings
  • View recently received directives
  • Navigate to all major app sections

Category Selection

Tailor your training to what matters most to you.

The Category Selection screen gives users full control over the types of directives they receive. Each category represents a different collection of situational awareness training directives that fall into a certain type. Users can toggle individual categories on or off so that automated directives are only drawn from the areas they want to focus on.

This screen also serves as the entry point to the Personal Directives list, allowing users to seamlessly move between managing categories and managing their own custom directives.

  • Toggle directive categories on or off individually
  • Focus your training on specific life areas
  • Access your Personal Directives list directly from this screen

Active Hours Customization

Receive directives only when it works for you.

Active Hours let users define exactly when during the day KIMinder360 will send them directives. Each day of the week can be configured independently, users can enable or disable active hours for a given day and set a custom start and end time for that day's window.

This feature ensures that training fits naturally into users' schedules without causing unwanted interruptions during work, sleep, or other committed times. Directives will only be delivered within the windows you define.

  • Enable or disable active hours per day of the week
  • Set custom start and end times for each day
  • Prevent interruptions outside your preferred schedule

Settings

Personalize your KIMinder360 experience.

The Settings screen is where users can fine-tune their KIMinder360 experience to match their preferences. From visual customization to how often they're prompted, this screen puts control in the user's hands.

Directive Frequency allows users to adjust how often they receive directives throughout the day, whether they want light, occasional notifications or a more consistent training flow. Theme selection lets users choose a visual style that feels comfortable for long-term use.

  • Theme selection to customize the app's appearance
  • Directive Frequency control for training intensity
  • Additional personalization and preference options

Personal Directives

Apply your training to what's unique in your own life.

Personal Directives allow users to go beyond the built-in directive library by creating their own. Whether it's a specific habit, a personal goal, or a situational reminder unique to your life, this screen lets you write, manage, and delete your own custom directives.

Custom directives are integrated directly into the automated training system, once created and enabled, they're included in the pool of directives that can be sent during active hours just like any built-in directive.

  • Create custom directives tailored to your personal goals
  • Edit or delete existing personal directives at any time
  • Custom directives integrate seamlessly into automated delivery

Info Screen

Start your journey with a clear understanding of KIMinder360.

The Info Screen is the first thing new users see when they open KIMinder360. It provides a thorough introduction to the app's purpose, explaining what KIMinder360 is, why situational awareness training matters, and how the directive-based system works to build that awareness over time.

The screen walks through each component of the training experience so users enter the app with a clear mental model of what to expect. At the end of the Info Screen, users are presented with a disclaimer they must acknowledge and agree to before gaining access to the rest of the app.

  • Full description of KIMinder360 and its training philosophy
  • Breakdown of key components of the situational awareness experience
  • Required disclaimer and user agreement before app access

Directive History

View your entire history of directives.

Directive history is where the user can view all of the directives that have been sent to them. This allows users to review their responses to past directives, respond to recent ones, or just view more directives than are shown on the homescreen.

This screen shows a total count of how many directives the user has in their history as well as how many directives they have completed. Below that count there is a list of directives just as you would find on the home screen which starts by showing the most recent directive all the way to the oldest one on record. Just as on the home screen the user can click on these directives to respond to them.

  • Comprehensive list of all the users past directives
  • Quick access status of how many directives they have in total and completed
  • Easy way to access any directive that was sent to the user

Directive Response

Engage with directives on a deeper level.

The directive response screen is where the central activity of KIMinder360 happens. It is here that users are prompted to record their response to a directive and can view more information about it. They also have various other means of interaction at their disposal depending on their engagement level.

The screen contains the directive, when it was sent, and the category it belongs to as its core information. Below that the user may have the option to mark it as complete or enter a free response depending on their interaction level (Lower levels have less modes of interaction available). Then the user can share the directive with friends to hear their response or delete the directive from their history if they have no interest in keeping it.

  • Record of Directive Information
  • Multiple Methods through which to respond
  • Ability to share and manage the directive
